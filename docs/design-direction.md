# RoadStar design direction

The operator selected **Precision transport — graphite, ivory, signal orange, tactile route diagrams** on September10,2026 and requested a complete redesign of web, native mobile, demo and pitch deck.

Active brief: [Precision transport](design/precision-transport.md). Reference study: [inspected references](design/references.md). Original media provenance: [asset manifest](design/assets.json).

This replaces the earlier blue/amber uniform-panel design and its blanket no-motion rule. Keep the implemented operational semantics, explicit human approvals, source provenance and truthful synthetic/live distinctions. The design uses original studio road artwork for brand storytelling and actual truck routes for operational maps. Full design verification is in progress; screenshots and independent critique must match the final build before it is marked complete.
