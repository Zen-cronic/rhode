# Corridor replay visual critique

All three assessments used fresh, screenshot-only contexts. The critics did not inspect code or functional evidence. Functional acceptance is recorded separately in `verification.json`.

## Initial assessment — 7.3/10

The critic praised the dark route stage, telemetry band, restrained orange route, grid and inset evidence card. It found weak mobile header/control hierarchy, raw identifiers that dominated the source panel and an oversized timeline focus/control treatment.

The first repair introduced a three-way narrow view selector, a clearer mobile header, compact visible identifiers with full accessible values, and a restrained focus treatment. It also removed an ornamental spinning truck, fixed the plan-camera orientation and kept the diagram readout clear of its marker.

## First reassessment — 7.4/10

The critic found a coherent industrial control-room aesthetic and strong charcoal/orange telemetry core. It found the route too small within the grid, replay controls spread into separate fragments and the smallest metadata too low-contrast.

The second repair tightened the route camera, enlarged the truck, reduced grid density and contrast, aligned the playback controls into one grid, and raised supporting type size and contrast while trimming repeated narrow-layout padding.

## Final reassessment — 8.0/10

The final critic described the system as a restrained telemetry console with warm ivory, charcoal, safety orange, monospaced metadata and geometric route visualization. Its strongest finding was the disciplined responsive composition: the wide event overlay, four-column telemetry band and horizontal controls become a coherent narrow stack and 2x2 instrument grid without losing identity.

Remaining gaps:

1. The route scene still lacks vehicle detail and restrained geographic cues.
2. Some narrow metadata and footer copy remains small and low-contrast.
3. The narrow playback section has several similarly weighted rows.

The assessment cap is exhausted. Status is `VISUAL_NEEDS_REVISION`; no 9/10 claim is made.
