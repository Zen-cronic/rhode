## Eight territories explored

1. **Precision transport (selected):** graphite structural rails, warm ivory work surfaces, signal-orange active route; oversized condensed headings with small tabular operational labels; a shallow physical route sculpture becomes a film/deck brand object.
2. **Editorial atlas:** warm paper, oversized city names, atlas index rails, restrained serif headlines; route geometry is the central page composition rather than a card.
3. **Night operations:** deep graphite, bright ivory time values, sparse amber/orange events, a wide low-contrast map with one illuminated active load.
4. **Freight wayfinding:** transit-sign typography, destination bands and timetable rows, explicit route interchange marks, sharply organized native action panels.
5. **Machined instrument:** pale anodized surfaces and fine calibration rules, one recessed dark instrument panel, large mechanical-style values; tactile depth confined to a small number of objects.
6. **Continental panorama:** full-bleed freight landscapes and oversized typography in deck/film; operation UI becomes a quiet full-height split workspace with horizon-like time axis.
7. **Dispatch ledger:** paper-and-ink trip tickets, firm ruled lines, compact proofs and signatures; tactile evidence drawers and manifests, little container chrome.
8. **Route installation:** a sculptural ribbon road with interchangeable vehicle blocks and a moving orange trajectory; product transition maps actual selected-trip state to an explanatory diagram.

Recommendation: selected precision transport is strongest across all four media. Borrow editorial scale for film/deck, and the route object as a recurring motif. Web and native must still orient the user with one current assignment and one prominent next action. Do not make a dense dashboard into a marketing hero.

## Implementation implications for coordinator

- Web: graphic dark structural rail; ivory spatial work area; route/load naming carries hierarchy; reduce uniform card framing. The active incident gets a distinct contrast plane and recovery comparison shares a precise time baseline.
- Native driver: route/destination headline, compact status, immediate next action; manifest and document detail continue below. Dispatcher mode: exception-first summary and explicit approval action, with the same route geometry and typographic rhythm.
- Film: original tactile road sculpture establishes the motif, match-cut to actual route/recovery UI, close on verified result. Motion should reveal the state transition and preserve static/reduced-motion frames.
- Deck: large chapter numerals, sharply scaled titles, route segment as orientation, authentic screenshots in generous compositions. A full-screen image or original object on two key slides can carry more identity than decorative repeated gradients.
